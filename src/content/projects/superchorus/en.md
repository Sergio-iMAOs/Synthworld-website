---
summary: Advanced polyphonic chorus VST plugin
---

SuperChorus is a stereo chorus audio plugin that layers multiple modulated delay voices across the stereo field to produce spatially immersive time-modulation effects. Each voice applies a short, LFO-modulated delay to the input signal, and voices are symmetrically distributed in the stereo panorama with evenly spaced modulation phases.

Voices positioned further from the stereo centre can receive progressively stronger nonlinear distortion, while an optional bit-crushing stage reduces the bit depth of all voices uniformly. The result is a highly configurable effect that combines classic chorus modulation, spatial placement, harmonic saturation, and lo-fi texture within a single plugin.
