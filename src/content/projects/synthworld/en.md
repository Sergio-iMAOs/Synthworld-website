Synthworld is a work in progress C++ plugin synthesiser making framework that allows to develop independent custom processors with their custom GUI and automatically incorporate them into a fully flexible deep polyphonic synthesiser.

## Modulate anything

Synthworld provides a fully flexible modulation matrix that allows all kind of modulations. Any processor can modulate any parameter.

Make nested modulations, self/feedback modulations, modulate the modulation amount of any modulation.

## Just worry about your DSP algorithms

Define your DSP processors and Synthworld will incorporate them into the synthesiser, providing communication with the GUI, polyphony, voice management, and much more. Everything smoothed, so you can forget about clicks and artifacts.

## Fully flexible and dynamic routing

Smoothly change the routing of your processors while playing. Any Directed Acyclic Graph is supported.
